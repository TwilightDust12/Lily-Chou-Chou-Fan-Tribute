Some films end.
This one stays.

A voice in your headphones. A name on a message board. A field so green it almost hurts to look at.

All About Lily Chou-Chou follows Yuichi, a teenager who finds refuge in a singer’s music and the strangers who love it. Beauty and cruelty exist painfully close together.

Find a familiar feeling ↗
光

The light

Noboru Shinoda’s images move between luminous fields and lonely screens. The landscape holds feelings the characters cannot say.

声

The voice

Salyu gives Lily her voice, with music by Takeshi Kobayashi. For Yuichi, listening becomes a way of finding somewhere to belong.
